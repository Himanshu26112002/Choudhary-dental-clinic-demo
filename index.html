Our Legacy of Care

A Commitment to Healthy Smiles Since 1997

For nearly three decades, Choudhary Dental Hospital & Multispeciality Clinic has served families across Rawatsar, Sardarshar, and Hanumangarh. What began with a mission to bring compassionate, reliable oral healthcare to rural and suburban Rajasthan has evolved into a fully equipped center for specialized dental treatments.

Led by Dr. Mahender Kumar (Jhorar), BDS, we blend clinical precision with anxiety-free dentistry. We treat patients like family, taking time to explain every radiograph, treatment choice, and preventive habit without rushed commercial pressure.

To provide accessible, high-caliber, and gentle dental procedures to patients of all age groups, eliminating dental pain while restoring function, natural aesthetics, and self-confidence.

To be Rajasthan’s gold standard for compassionate regional dental care—featuring painless endodontics, durable prosthetics, and digital diagnosis at affordable local pricing.

Procedure Guide

What Actually Happens During a Root Canal?

Far from painful, a modern root canal actually relieves severe pain. We numb the localized zone, gently clean the tiny inflamed pulp canal, sanitize with antiseptic solutions, and seal it permanently.

Preventive Fact

Why Scaling Prevents Tooth Loosening

Tartar calcifies under the gums and slowly dissolves supporting jawbone. Ultrasonic scaling does not weaken or thin teeth—it removes the infection destroying their biological anchors.

Recommended every 6–12 months

Parenting Care

Caring for Milk Teeth in Growing Children

"Baby teeth will fall out anyway" is a risky myth. Untreated cavities in milk teeth can infect developing permanent teeth beneath them and lead to severe orthodontic misalignment later in life.

Early habits save orthodontics cost
